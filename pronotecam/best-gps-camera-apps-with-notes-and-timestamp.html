Why Professional Geotagging Requires More Than a Generic Camera

While hundreds of photo apps populate the Google Play Store, field professionals in construction, real estate, land survey, and insurance need specific capabilities that ordinary camera apps fail to provide.

A truly useful GPS camera for work must satisfy four key requirements:

  1. Visible Watermark: Information must be stamped visibly on the picture pixels, not just hidden inside EXIF metadata that gets stripped when shared over WhatsApp or Slack.
  2. Custom Text Notes: The ability to add job notes, observation remarks, or project numbers before saving.
  3. High Sensor Accuracy: Visual feedback on satellite fix quality (GPS error margin).
  4. Offline Independence: Operating without Wi-Fi, cellular towers, or user logins.
