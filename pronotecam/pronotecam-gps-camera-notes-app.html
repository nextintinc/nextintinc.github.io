ProNoteCam solves this problem completely. ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How accurate is the GPS coordinate stamping in ProNoteCam?
ProNoteCam utilizes your Android device's high-precision GNSS/GPS chipset. Accuracy typically ranges from 2 to 5 meters under clear skies. ProNoteCam also displays the real-time accuracy metric on the viewfinder so you know when the GPS fix is optimal before snapping the picture.
Can I reuse notes I frequently use for different inspections?
Yes! ProNoteCam saves your recent notes so you can quickly apply frequent tags like "Foundational Inspection", "Pavement Survey", or "Client Unit #402" without having to retype them every single time.
Does ProNoteCam require internet access to stamp coordinates?
No! Satellite GPS signals operate independently of mobile data and Wi-Fi. ProNoteCam can capture, stamp, and organize your geotagged photos even in airplane mode or in remote mountain terrains.
Is ProNoteCam free to download and use?
Yes, ProNoteCam is completely free to install and use on Android devices directly from the Google Play Store with no hidden sign-up fees or subscription paywalls.
