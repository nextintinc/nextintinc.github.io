2. Udhari Book: Cash & Khata — Complete Toolkit for Indian Shopkeepers

💰

Udhari Book: Cash & Khata

Business & Finance · Offline Ledger

For kirana stores, traders, cloth merchants, and small service providers, managing daily transactions manually in a red paper bahi-khata leads to costly errors and awkward payment follow-ups. Udhari Book: Cash & Khata unifies four critical business utilities into a single, offline application that requires no login.

  • Cash Counter: Denomination breakdown for all Indian notes (₹2000, ₹500, ₹200, ₹100, ₹50, ₹20, ₹10) and coins with auto-total in lakh/crore format and words.
  • Daily Cash Register: Log Opening balance, Sales, Expenses, and auto-calculate Closing balance.
  • Customer Udhar Ledger: Record Diya (given) and Liya (received) entries and send polite reminders via WhatsApp in Hindi, Marathi, or English.
  • GST Calculator: Instant 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28% tax calculation with CGST/SGST split and MRP tax extraction.

🌸

Ladki Bahin Yojana eKYC & Status

However, thousands face common roadblocks: biometric eKYC failures, bank DBT linking discrepancies, and unverified beneficiary names. The Ladki Bahin Yojana eKYC App serves as a one-stop utility guide.

  • Beneficiary List Checking: Step-by-step guidance to search district and municipal beneficiary lists online.
  • Aadhar Biometric eKYC Steps: Clear instructions to complete Aadhaar OTP or CSC biometric verification without middlemen.
  • Payment Installment Schedules: Real-time updates on scheduled DBT disbursement dates and combined installment releases.
  • Direct Official Portal Links: Safe, secure navigation straight to legitimate state government servers.

7. Mahila Shetkari Pramanpatra App — Online Application & Subsidy Portal

🌾

Mahila Shetkari Pramanpatra App

Agriculture · Women Farmer Empowerment

Women farmers constitute over 60% of India's agricultural workforce, yet many miss out on state agricultural subsidies (drip irrigation, solar pumps, farm mechanization grants) due to the absence of independent land records in their name. The Mahila Shetkari Pramanpatra App provides a clear roadmap to obtain the official Woman Farmer Certificate.

  • Eligibility & Required Documents: Clear checklist of 7/12 extract, ration card, Aadhaar, and family consent forms.
  • Gram Sabha & Nagar Panchayat Approval: Understand how the certificate is verified and passed during local village councils.
  • Government Scheme Subsidies: Access up to 50%–90% government grants under RKVY, PMKSY drip irrigation, and crop protection schemes.
  • Download Certificate Online: Track Mahakrushi portal status and download the digital certificate safely.
