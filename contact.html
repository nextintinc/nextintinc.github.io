Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How do I report a bug in your app?

Use the contact form above or email us at sonawalesvijay@gmail.com. Please include your device name, Android version, and a description of the bug. Screenshots are very helpful!

❓ Can I request a new feature?

Absolutely! We love hearing from users. Send us your feature idea and we'll consider it for future updates.

❓ Is my data safe? Do you collect personal information?

All our apps are privacy-first. No login, no data upload, no tracking. Your data stays on your device. Read our full Privacy Policy.

❓ Are your apps really free?

Yes! All our apps are completely free to download and use. No hidden charges, no premium locks, no subscriptions.

❓ What is your typical response time?

We usually respond within 24 hours. For complex issues, it may take 2-3 business days.
