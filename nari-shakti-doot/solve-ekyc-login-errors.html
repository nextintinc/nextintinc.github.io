Nari Shakti Doot App: How to Solve Common eKYC and Login Errors in 2026

Don't let technical glitches delay your ₹1,500 monthly payment. Follow this troubleshooting guide to resolve all app errors instantly.

Millions of women in Maharashtra rely on the Nari Shakti Doot App to access the Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana. However, due to massive server traffic, many users experience issues with logging in, receiving OTPs, or completing their eKYC.

If you are facing these issues, don't panic. Below is a comprehensive guide to fixing the most common errors in the app.

1. "App is Not Responding" or White Screen Error

Because the app communicates with central government servers, high traffic during peak hours (usually 10 AM to 2 PM) can cause the app to freeze.

  • Solution: Close the app completely from your recent apps menu. Go to your phone's Settings > Apps > Nari Shakti Doot > Storage > Clear Cache. Restart the app.
  • Pro Tip: Try logging in late at night or early in the morning when server traffic is minimal.

2. Not Receiving Login OTP

The SMS gateway sometimes gets overloaded, delaying the OTP delivery to your mobile number.

  • Check Your Network: Ensure you have full cellular reception, not just Wi-Fi.
  • Check DND Settings: Ensure your phone is not actively blocking transactional SMS through a "Do Not Disturb" (DND) feature.
  • Wait it out: Instead of clicking "Resend OTP" multiple times in a row, wait at least 3 minutes before trying again. Hitting resend multiple times flags your number for spam on the server.

3. eKYC Fails: "Aadhaar Details Mismatch"

To receive the ₹1,500 monthly installment, your eKYC must be successful. If the app shows a mismatch error, it means the name on your application doesn't perfectly match your Aadhaar database.

  • Solution: Ensure your name spelling exactly matches what is printed on your Aadhaar card. Even a missing middle name or a space can trigger this error.
  • Bank Linking: Ensure your Aadhaar is officially seeded/linked to your bank account with NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India). You can check your seeding status at your local bank branch.
Important: The government transfers funds exclusively via Aadhaar Based Payment System (ABPS). If your bank account is not Aadhaar-seeded, your eKYC will fail, and the payment will bounce.

4. "Old App Version" Error

The Maharashtra government frequently updates the app to fix security bugs. If you are using an older version, the server will block your login attempts.

  • Solution: Uninstall the old app. Visit the Google Play Store, search for "Nari Shakti Doot", and download the latest verified version. Your data is tied to your phone number, so you won't lose your application status.

Need to check your application status?

Ensure you have the latest updated version of the Nari Shakti Doot app to check if your Ladki Bahin Yojana payment is approved.
