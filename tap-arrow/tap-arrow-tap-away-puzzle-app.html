💡 Top Solving Strategies

  • Peel from the Outside-In: Always scan the perimeter first. Arrows facing outward with an open boundary are your safest initial moves.
  • Trace the Flight Line: Before tapping, visually trace the arrow's entire path to ensure no hidden tip or flank blocks it.
  • Create Domino Openings: Look for "keystone" arrows — clearing one stubborn arrow often frees up three or four adjacent arrows simultaneously.
  • Save Hints for Knot Puzzles: Reserve hint power-ups for dense clusters where arrows face inward toward each other.

❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about playing Tap Arrow: Tap Away Puzzle.

1. What is the main objective of Tap Arrow: Tap Away Puzzle?

The goal is to clear the board by sending every arrow away in the direction it points. Because arrows cannot move through other arrows, your task is to discover the exact sequence that allows each arrow to fly away freely.

2. What happens if I tap an arrow whose path is blocked?

If an arrow's trajectory is obstructed, it will wiggle and produce a gentle haptic vibration to let you know it cannot move yet. You must first clear the blocking arrow before this one can launch.

3. Can I play this game completely offline?

Yes! All puzzle levels, audio feedback, haptics, and mechanics work 100% offline. You only need internet if you choose to synchronize your high scores to the global leaderboard or claim online challenge rewards.

4. How do the Hint and Undo features work?

If you make an accidental move or tap the wrong arrow, you can hit the Undo button to roll back. If you are ever perplexed by a complex board, tapping Hint immediately highlights an arrow that is fully clear and ready to be tapped away.

5. Are there any paywalls, subscriptions, or login requirements?

None. NextInt Inc builds privacy-first, accessible apps. Tap Arrow requires no account signup, collects no personal identity data, and gives you free access to the entire game experience.
