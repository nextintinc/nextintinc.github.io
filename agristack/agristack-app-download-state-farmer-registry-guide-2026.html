AgriStack App Download & State Farmer Registry Guide (2026)

How to download official AgriStack mobile apps, access state portals (MHFR, RJFRC, GJFR, MPFR), complete Aadhaar e-KYC, and generate your 11-digit Digital Farmer ID card.

The Government of India, in coordination with State Agriculture and Revenue Departments, has launched AgriStack (डिजिटल कृषी मिशन) nationwide. Under this flagship Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI), every cultivating landholder is being issued a unique 11-digit Digital Farmer ID linked directly to Aadhaar and digitized land records (7/12, Jamabandi, Khasra, and RoR).

Whether you are waiting for your next PM-KISAN ₹2,000 installment, applying for PMFBY 1-rupee crop insurance, or seeking subsidized seeds, drip irrigation grants, and KCC loan waivers, AgriStack registration is now compulsory.

⚠️ Mandatory Deadline Alert: Several states have made AgriStack Farmer ID mandatory for upcoming welfare disbursements. Farmers without an authenticated Farmer ID risk payment delays on their DBT-linked bank accounts.

3. Step-by-Step AgriStack e-KYC & Registration Process

Completing your AgriStack registration takes less than 10 minutes if you have the following prerequisites ready:

  • ✅ Aadhaar Card with mobile number linked and active for OTP
  • ✅ Land Documents: 7/12 extract (Maharashtra), Jamabandi/Khatauni (Rajasthan/UP), or Village Form 8A
  • ✅ Bank Passbook: Aadhaar-seeded NPCI/DBT active bank account

Step 1: Aadhaar Authentication (e-KYC)

Open your state portal (or launcher app) and select "Farmer Registration". Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number. An OTP will be dispatched to your UIDAI-registered mobile number. Enter the OTP to verify your legal identity and address.

Step 2: Land Plot Linking (Geo-Referenced Survey)

Select your District, Taluka/Tehsil, and Village. Enter your Survey Number, Gut Number, or Khasra Number. The system automatically fetches your land area in hectares and compares the owner name with your Aadhaar name.

Step 3: Verification by Revenue Official (Talathi / Patwari)

Once submitted, your application status appears as "Pending Village Officer Verification". The local Patwari or Talathi verifies the physical boundary and tenancy. Once approved, your status turns Green.

4. How to Download the 11-Digit Digital Farmer ID Card (PDF)

Once verification is completed:

  1. Log in to your state AgriStack portal using your registered mobile number and OTP.
  2. Click on the "Farmer Profile" or "Digital Farmer ID" tab.
  3. Your 11-digit alphanumeric Farmer ID (e.g., MH-2026-XXXXXXX) will be displayed alongside your photo and total registered land holding.
  4. Click "Download Farmer Card (PDF)". You can print and laminate this digital ID card for CSC center visits, fertilizer subsidies, and bank loan documentation.

5. Troubleshooting Common AgriStack App & Portal Errors

Here are tested solutions to the most common issues reported by farmers:

  • "Land Record Not Found": Ensure that your name spelling on the computerized 7/12 (Digital Satbara) matches your Aadhaar card. If there is a mismatch, visit your local Tehsil office for name correction in the revenue records.
  • "OTP Not Received": If OTP is delayed during peak hours (11 AM to 4 PM), attempt registration in the evening or early morning when the UIDAI gateway traffic is low.
  • "Joint Account / Co-Owner Issue": If land is shared among siblings, each co-owner must register separately with their specific share in hectares (Hissa).
