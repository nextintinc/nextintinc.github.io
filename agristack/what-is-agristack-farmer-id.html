What is AgriStack? The Ultimate Guide to Farmer Registration in 2026

Everything you need to know about India's digital agriculture initiative and how to get your unique Farmer ID.

If you are a farmer in India, you have likely heard the term AgriStack buzzing around in agricultural offices and news channels. But what exactly is it, and why is the government making it mandatory?

In this guide, we break down what the AgriStack initiative is, why the Farmer ID is crucial for your future, and how you can easily access your state's registration portal.

What is AgriStack?

AgriStack is a Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) created by the Government of India. Think of it as a unified digital ecosystem where every farmer’s data—such as their identity (Aadhaar), land records, and crop sown data—is verified and stored securely in one place.

The core component of AgriStack is the Farmers' Registry. When you register on the portal, you are assigned a unique Farmer ID, much like an Aadhaar card but specifically for agricultural purposes.

Why is the Farmer ID Mandatory?

Historically, to get benefits from various schemes like PM-KISAN, Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY), or Kisan Credit Cards (KCC), farmers had to submit physical copies of their land records and Aadhaar cards repeatedly to different departments.

With AgriStack, your Farmer ID serves as a single source of truth. Once you are in the system, benefits can be transferred to you directly (DBT) without the need for endless paperwork or verification delays.

Key Benefits of Registering:

  • Faster PM-KISAN Payments: Eradicates the problem of delayed installments due to name or land record mismatches.
  • Easy Access to Loans & Insurance: Banks and insurance companies can digitally verify your land records instantly.
  • Targeted Advisories: Receive weather and crop advisories specific to the exact location of your farm.

The Challenge of Finding State Portals

Because agriculture is a state subject in India, the central government has mandated that every state build its own portal to connect to the national AgriStack network. This has created massive confusion.

For instance, farmers in Maharashtra have to use mhfr.agristack.gov.in, while farmers in Bihar must use a completely different URL. Searching for these portals online often leads to confusing government sites or, worse, fake fraudulent websites.
