❓ Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Miso, screen credits, and permissions.

What makes Miso different from other screen time blockers?
Most screen blockers rely on aggressive lockouts, harsh warnings, or guilt-inducing shame. Miso treats digital wellbeing with compassion. It uses warm cozy art, a sleeping cat companion, and a positive movement-earning loop. Instead of feeling restricted, you feel empowered to step away, stretch, and return with intention.
How does camera-based rep counting work? Is it private?
Miso uses lightweight, on-device computer vision models to count exercise repetitions (push-ups, squats, jumping jacks, sit-ups, and planks). The video feed is analyzed frame-by-frame entirely inside your phone's memory and is discarded immediately. No video frames, photographs, or recordings are ever saved to storage or transmitted over the internet.
How do earned minutes and credits work?
One credit equals one minute of extra app time. You earn minutes by doing movement breaks (1 rep = 1 min; 30s plank = 1 min). Earned minutes stay stored locally on your device. If you ever need immediate minutes without exercise, optional 30-credit packs are available in Google Play.
Why does Miso need Android Accessibility permission?
Android requires Accessibility service access so that Miso can detect when you open an app on your protected list. When you have no remaining minutes, Miso displays its cozy resting break screen. Miso's service does not request, view, or record screen contents, text inputs, notifications, or keystrokes.
