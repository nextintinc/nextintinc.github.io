🎮 How To Play

Simple Rules, Fast Fun

Learn in seconds, master over time. Quick rounds, pure skill.

🃏

Start with 5 Cards

You begin each round with 5 cards in your hand. The clock starts ticking!

🔄

Draw & Match

Draw from deck or open pile. Match 2 cards of same value to form a Jodi (pair).

🏆

Win the Round

Lowest deadwood wins when timer ends. Finish all cards into Jodis to win instantly!
