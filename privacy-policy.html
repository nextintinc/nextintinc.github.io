Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NextInt Inc ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website nextintinc.in and use our Android applications.

📅 Last Updated: September 16, 2026 🏢 NextInt Inc (Vijay Santosh Sonawale)

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Website Visitors

When you visit nextintinc.in, we may collect the following information through third-party analytics services:

  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and navigation patterns
  • Browser type, device type, operating system, and screen resolution
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level, not exact address)
  • Referring website or search query that brought you here

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email, phone number, or address through passive browsing. If you voluntarily send us an email through the contact form, we receive only the information you choose to share.

1.2 Android Applications

Our Android apps (Udhari Book, Car Kundali, and GovFitAI) are designed with a privacy-first approach:

  • No login or account creation required — ever
  • No personal data is uploaded to any server or cloud
  • All your data stays on your device — we never access it
  • No third-party tracking SDKs that collect personal data

🔒 Our Promise: Your business data, car diagnostics data, and quiz progress in our apps are stored only on your device. We have zero access to this data.

2. How We Use Information

The limited analytics data we collect from website visits is used solely to:

  • Understand which pages and content are most helpful to visitors
  • Improve website design, navigation, and user experience
  • Identify and fix technical issues
  • Understand our audience demographics at an aggregate level

We do not sell, rent, or share any collected data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses the following third-party services that may set cookies:

  • Google Analytics (GA4): Used to understand website traffic and usage patterns. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect anonymous usage data. You can opt out by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Google Fonts: Used to load typefaces. Google may log font requests.
  • Font Awesome (CDN): Used to load icon fonts via Cloudflare CDN.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may not affect the core functionality of this website.

4. Third-Party Links

Our website and apps may contain links to third-party websites, including:

  • Google Play Store (for app downloads)
  • Government scheme portals (for informational articles)
  • Other educational and reference resources

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external websites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

5. Children's Privacy

Our website and apps are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

6. Data Security

Our website is served over HTTPS (SSL/TLS encryption) to ensure secure data transmission. Since we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, the risk of data breach from our side is minimal. App data remains securely on your personal device.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request information about what data (if any) we hold about you
  • Request deletion of any personal data you've shared with us via email
  • Opt out of Google Analytics tracking
  • Uninstall our apps at any time — all local data is deleted with uninstall

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
