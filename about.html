Our Mission

We believe technology should be accessible, simple, and respectful of user privacy. Many Indian users — especially small shopkeepers and rural users — need apps that:

  • Work on entry-level Android phones (2GB RAM or less)
  • Don't require constant internet connectivity
  • Don't force account creation or OTP verification
  • Support local languages like Hindi and Marathi
  • Are completely free to use without hidden charges

That's exactly what we build at NextInt Inc.
